Ported from it: the Reviews rating summary, the yellow Three's Lemonade heading, the Drinks filter in Pics, Meet the Team after Visit, tap-to-love and (Batch 2) the Shippy chat. Not ported: V6's extra menu cards (Lomi, Siopao, Beef broccoli, Beef ampalaya), its sample reviews, its sample team names (Marco Villanueva, Jun Dela Rosa, Bea Mendoza are not staff), its "Tapsilog is the best seller" claim and its "Open now" wording. v1.3.0 final batch: `context/design/PONGSKILOG Landing Page v7.html` supersedes V6 where they differ. Ported: the smaller "Visit us" heading in place of "Come hungry.", no big bottom "Message us" link (the Info and contact card keeps its Facebook button), the online kiosk section with the sample ticket #024 (an illustration, `role="img"`) and its "Choose your branch" sheet (a bottom sheet in the last 699px block), and the seven Three's Lemonade posters. ## Team portraits are framed in the browser

Website → Team: choosing or replacing a portrait opens `TeamPhotoCropDialog`, whose frame is the public 3:4 card (role tab, shade, name, bio, role, placeholders included). Drag with a mouse or finger, pinch, the slider, ± or the arrow keys; zoom 1 just covers the card (never letterboxed), up to 4x (`lib/website-team.ts`). The framed part is drawn to a canvas (at most 810x1080, never upscaled) and that JPEG goes through the existing photo endpoint, so the image pipeline, WebP, S3, audit and permissions are unchanged and no crop data is stored. The original never leaves the browser (accepted up to 20 MB); the server's 5 MB limit applies to the framed upload.

## Shippy knows the visible team, and never claims an item is available

- **Team.** `ShippyTeam` reads only visible cards that have a name: a hidden card, a "Name to follow" placeholder and staff accounts are out of reach. A question only about a member's name (accents folded; one letter off allowed from five letters, but only in a question that asks who or about someone, so "Price?" never finds a "Prince"), a role asked with who/sino, or the team is answered locally (`source: team`, after the menu and before FAQs) with `kind: person` rows (portrait, role under the name) and the `team` action (`#team`). A name that is Taglish filler (such as "May") cannot be matched. A longer question goes to Gemini with a TEAM line of the visible members, sent only when the conversation names a member or asks who or about the team; Gemini's `items` may name a visible member, nothing else. With no note and no visible member's name or role mentioned, a who question still gets "no info" locally.
- **Availability.** What is left tonight is not known here. A stock question about a menu item (`ShippyKnowledge::STOCK`) with no matching Owner note is answered locally with its menu price and "message us to be sure" (`source: menu`, no Gemini). With notes Gemini answers, but a Gemini reply that claims stock about a menu item (or any stock claim when it had no notes) is discarded for that honest answer. A claim is a clause with a stock word and no word that leaves it to be checked (`claimsStock()`): "message us to check if it's available" is kept.

## Tap-to-love is one hourly count, shared by the page and the Owner

`landing_loves` holds taps per Asia/Manila date and clock hour, nothing about the visitor. The landing total is the sum of all rows and Website → Analytics → Heart Taps is the sum of a period, so they always agree; never add a second counter. `LandingLove::add()` is one atomic upsert and ignores a batch key seen in the last ten minutes. The page sends taps as batches (at most `MAX_TAPS`, about one request per two seconds, same key on retry) through the `landing-love` limiter, reads the total with the page and each saved batch, and never polls. The "Tap tap!" invitation is not remembered: it returns on every load.

Batch 2 fixed two confirmed defects without realtime: taps still waiting for their batch were lost when the page was left (six quick taps then a reload saved one), and a reload or Back showed a total older than what the visitor had seen. Now `createLoveQueue().leave()` hands every unsaved tap over on `pagehide`, on a hidden tab and on unmount with a keepalive request (`sendLoveOnLeave`, same key for a batch already travelling), and the highest count a tab has shown is kept in `sessionStorage` (`loveFloor`, a number only) so the display never goes backwards. That storage code lives in `lib/landing-love.ts`; the component itself stays free of storage and cookies.

## Website is Analytics, Reviews, Team and Shippy

The Owner workspace is labelled Website (route and component names stay `workspaces.website-analytics`). It needs a business-wide scope and either `reports.view` (Analytics) or website content access (Reviews, Team, Shippy: active Owner or Super Admin with `settings.manage`); the server sends each tab's data only to accounts that may use it. Review moderation moved here from Settings and must not be duplicated there. Heart taps ride in the existing `report` prop and its existing refresh: no new chart, poll or channel.

## The landing team is managed in Website → Team

Batch 2 (supersedes "exactly six cards"): members are added (optional portrait in the same request), edited, hidden, moved one place earlier or later, and deleted after a confirmation, up to `LandingTeamMember::MAX_MEMBERS` (30); the six rows of the first migration stay as they are. `position` is no longer unique: a reorder sends every id once (`ReorderLandingTeamRequest`) and the server renumbers 1..n in one transaction, as it does after a delete. ## Shippy answers only from the published menu

Batch 2. `resources/js/lib/landing-menu.json` is the one published menu: `landing.ts` imports it as `MENU` and `ShippyKnowledge` reads the same file, so a name or price is never typed twice. Shippy knows only that menu, the scheduled hours in Asia/Manila, the address, the action destinations (`ShippyKnowledge::ACTIONS`: menu, maps, messenger, reviews and, since V7, team) and best sellers derived from the last 30 days of committed, non-voided sales mapped onto published names (at least 10 sold; a POS product that is not published is never named). It never claims the store is open right now or that an item is available: the schedule is all that is known. The POS catalog, customers, orders and staff are out of reach by construction.

Gemini is called only from `ShippyAssistant` with the key in an `x-goog-api-key` header (`config('services.gemini')`, never in a URL, a log, a prop or the UI). Its JSON answer is untrusted: tags stripped, any link rejected, items resolved against the menu, actions against the allowlist, a card dearer than a stated budget removed, and a peso amount in the text must be the price of an attached card or a number the guest typed, otherwise the whole answer is discarded. Before a call, in order: key present, circuit breaker closed, one call per guest at a time, global per-minute ceiling, atomic daily quota (`ShippyUsage::reserve`). Any refusal, failure, timeout or unusable answer falls back to `ShippyKnowledge::answer()`; no other model or key is ever tried, so changing `GEMINI_MODEL` is the Owner's decision, never an automatic fallback. One guest's repeated question is answered once per minute. Since the instant-answer engine (below) Gemini is no longer asked first, and the ten-minute preset cache is gone: every preset is answered locally.

The model is `gemini-3.1-flash-lite`, chosen by measurement on the free-tier key on 2026-10-14 (the user asked for the latency fix). The wait is Google's free-tier serving, not the request: `gemini-3.5-flash-lite` took 31 to 49 seconds in 4 of 5 calls, 49 of them for a three-token reply to a ten-token prompt, with no thinking tokens and all of it in `server-timing`; dropping the schema or the facts changes nothing, so do not trim the prompt to chase speed. `gemini-3.1-flash-lite` answered 12 of 16 calls in 3 to 7 seconds (3 timeouts, one 503). `gemini-3-flash-preview` is faster when it answers (1.5 to 3 seconds) but timed out three times in a row; `gemini-3.5-flash` and `gemini-3.8-flash` answered 503 and every 2.5 model answers 404 "no longer available to new users". The request sends `thinkingConfig.thinkingLevel: minimal`: thinking counts against `maxOutputTokens`, and with it on `gemini-3-flash-preview` spent the limit thinking and returned a cut-off answer. A 2.5 model would need `thinkingBudget` instead (`thinkingBudget: 0` is a 400 on Gemini 3). The 8-second timeout stays: never raise it to wait for a slow provider, because a waiting request holds a PHP worker the POS shares. While Gemini keeps failing or timing out the circuit breaker's pauses double (5, 10, 20, 40, 60 minutes) and one failed attempt starts the next pause, so a slow provider costs about one request an hour; the first answer that arrives resets it. The two list prices in `config('services.gemini')` belong to the model: change them with it.

Nothing a guest types is stored or logged: `shippy_usage` is five counters per Manila date, the browser keeps the conversation in memory only, and the Privacy Notice discloses that questions go to Google's Gemini service (`CustomerReview::NOTICE_VERSION` was bumped). The mascot shown is a 256 px WebP made from `public/images/shippy.png`; the 2 MB source is never requested by the page. Website → Shippy has the on/off switch (`shippy_settings`, audited) and the usage figures with an estimated cost: read with the page, refreshed on request, never polled.

## Shippy answers locally first; Gemini only when nothing local is sure

User request (v1.3.0, "Shippy knowledge and speed"). `ShippyAssistant::reply()` tries, in order, and reports which one answered in `source`: `menu` (`ShippyKnowledge::verified()`), `team` (`ShippyTeam`, since V7), `faq` and `markdown` (`ShippyKnowledgeBase`), `ai` (Gemini), `fallback` (`ShippyKnowledge::answer()`). The first three make no request to anyone; measured on the local server on 2026-10-14 they took 4 to 6 ms inside the engine (28 ms on the request that rebuilt the cache with a 73 KB document), against 4.5 to 8 seconds for Gemini.

- **Verified means the facts answer the whole question.** `verified()` returns the rule answer only when every word is filler (`FILLER`) or belongs to the one topic that matched (`TOPICS`, `CATEGORIES`, item words). "Open ba kayo ngayon?" is local; "Open ba kayo pag holiday?", "May open mic ba kayo?", a comparison, a quantity, availability and anything with a pronoun for an earlier turn are not. Do not loosen this by adding content words to `FILLER`: a wrong instant answer is worse than a slow right one. Order, payment and unknown topics are never verified, so an FAQ can answer them. A bare "magkano?" is verified only without history. An alternation in `TOPICS` lists the longer phrase first (`best seller|bestseller|best`), otherwise the leftover word blocks the match.
- **An FAQ or a heading must be clearly the question.** Words are compared as sets after filler removal, plural stripping and a small Taglish synonym table (`SAME`), one typo allowed in words of five letters or more. Instant needs likeness of at least `INSTANT` (0.7) and a lead of `MARGIN` (0.15) over the next entry; between two entries nobody is picked. The Owner widens a match by adding other wordings, not by lowering the numbers.
- **Gemini gets only matching notes.** `notes()` sends at most three FAQs or sections that share a third of the question's words, 500 characters each, in an `OWNER NOTES` block declared as data. A peso figure in the reply must still be a card price, a number the guest typed or a number in those notes.
- **The published menu is the only source of menu prices.** A document line that names a menu item and a peso amount is dropped (`withoutMenuPrices`), and a price question is verified before any FAQ is looked at.
- **A document is data.** `ShippyMarkdown` keeps headings and plain text only (no HTML, scripts, link targets or front matter) and drops lines that address the assistant. Documents are a `text` column in PostgreSQL, at most 10 of 100 KB: there is no file on a disk and no route that returns one; Website → Shippy receives the name, size, section count and date. No embeddings, vector store or extra service.
- **The cache is dropped by the models.** `ShippyFaq` and `ShippyDocument` forget `shippy:knowledge` after commit on every save and delete, so any write path invalidates it; do not add a TTL or a second cache key.
- **Test Shippy is the real engine.** `ShippyController::test` calls the same `reply()` (same quota, breaker and per-minute ceiling, own `shippy-test` limiter), works while the chat is off, stores nothing, and returns the engine's milliseconds. Only `ai` is labelled an AI answer.

## Question words never pick an answer; the engine owns the language

Manual QA fixes (v1.3.0): "sino si" was answered about Shamarra because the FAQ "Sino si Shamarra?" shared the bare word "sino" and went to Gemini as a note.

- **A match needs a word that says what the question is about.** `ShippyKnowledgeBase::QUESTION` (who, what, where, when, how, why, which, price, …) never counts on its own: `closest()` and `notes()` both require a shared `identifying()` word. A question with none is asked back by Shippy's own rules ("Sino yung gusto mong malaman?") when it opens the chat or stops on a particle (`ShippyAssistant::DANGLING`); a short follow-up such as "bakit?" still goes to Gemini. A "sino/who" question with no matching note is answered "no info" locally: Gemini never guesses who a person is. These rule answers report `source: fallback`.
- **Words are compared after a light Tagalog normaliser.** `root()` drops a verb prefix (`PREFIXES`) and a repeated first syllable while four letters remain, on both sides of every comparison; `SAME` maps synonyms, including sino→who. The cache key is versioned (`shippy:knowledge:2`): bump it whenever `terms()` changes, or old prepared terms stay in Redis forever.
- **Language is decided in code, not by documents.** `ShippyLanguage`: an explicit request ("Talk in English", "Taglish ulit") wins and travels back as `reply.preference`, which the landing chat and Test Shippy send with the next question (`preference` on the request; Start over clears it); otherwise a message with English function words and no Tagalog marker is English, everything else (including a dish name alone) is casual Taglish. Every local answer in `ShippyKnowledge` exists in both languages; Gemini gets one language rule. An uploaded style guide is reference data only.
- **A Tagalog FAQ or section asked in English is reworded by Gemini once.** `ShippyAssistant::english()` goes through the same guards as any Gemini call, sends only the Owner's text, rejects a figure the original lacks, a link or leftover Tagalog, and keeps the result 30 days under the hash of the source text (`shippy:english:*`, Owner text only, never guest text). It is reported as `ai`; without Gemini the approved text is shown as written. A Taglish reply may use an English FAQ as written: rewording every English FAQ would make most questions slow.

## A knowledge change without a server answer is confirmed, not failed

The "result is unconfirmed" upload error was a request the server committed after the browser stopped waiting (the dev server is single-worker on Windows, so an upload can queue behind a Gemini test question). `useKnowledgeChange` now treats only an HTTP error as a failure: with no response it re-reads `shippy` and asks the caller's `saved` check (an upload by stored name and exact size, `shippyUploadSaved`), then shows success, "not saved", or unconfirmed only if that read also fails. The server makes the retry harmless: the same file name with the same text, or the same FAQ question and answer, is already saved and returns 200 without a second row or audit entry; the same name with different text is still refused.
